Who are we

WeKwikGene is a plasmid repository run by Westlake Laboratory based in the XiHu district of Hangzhou, Zhejiang province, China.

WeKwikGene facilitates the sharing of high-quality scientific materials, research reproducibility, and open science by archiving, annotating, and distributing DNA-based research reagents and associated data to scientists in China.
