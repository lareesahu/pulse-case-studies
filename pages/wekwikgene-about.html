Accelerating discovery by improving access to plasmids

WeKwikGene facilitates the sharing of high-quality scientific materials, research reproducibility, and open science by archiving and distributing DNA-based research reagents and associated data to scientists in China.

About WeKwikGene

WeKwikGene is a plasmid repository that stores, verifies, annotates, and distributes high-quality plasmids to the scientific community across China.

Each plasmid in WeKwikGene’s repository is sequenced, annotated, and verified to ensure high quality and reliability. The searchable online database enables fast and efficient navigation through plasmid catalogs and provides full plasmid sequences with annotations.

WeKwikGene is a Research Platform at the Westlake Laboratory of Life Sciences and Biomedicine in Hangzhou, China.

Why WeKwikGene?

WeKwikGene is committed to providing Chinese scientists with quick access to high-quality plasmids.

1.

Efficient Distribution

WeKwikGene is a local plasmid repository; every request placed within China should reach its destination within 2–3 business days.

2.

Reliable Quality

All plasmids are diligently sequenced and annotated by our team of scientists at Westlake Laboratory to ensure the quality is consistent and reliable.

3.

Non-Profit

WeKwikGene is a non-profit research platform for sharing DNA-based resources amongst scientists in China. Both our community and repository are ever-growing.

Promote your research and manage your resources

WeKwikGene helps improve the visibility of your research and increase citations for your publications while keeping your valuable plasmids organized and archived for the future.
