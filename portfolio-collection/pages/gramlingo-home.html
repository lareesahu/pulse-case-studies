FAQ

Questions, answered

Is GramLingo really free?
Yes — the first grammar world is completely free, no credit card, no signup wall. You can start playing in under 10 seconds. Future worlds may have a small unlock cost.
What makes it different from Duolingo or grammar textbooks?
Textbooks explain rules then test you. Duolingo drills vocabulary with gamified streaks. GramLingo does neither — it turns grammar itself into a quest. Every question teaches through instant per-option feedback, and your mistakes auto-build a spaced-review Error Log. No drills, no flashcards, no "you lost your streak" anxiety.
Can I learn in my own language?
Yes — switch between English, 中文 and Español anytime, even mid-lesson. Explanations adapt to your language instantly. Perfect for learners who think in Chinese or Spanish but want to master English grammar.
I'm preparing for IELTS / CET-4 / gaokao — will this help?
Absolutely. The 12 worlds cover the exact grammar points these exams test — relative clauses, conditionals, passive voice, modals, and more. Each world's phases get progressively harder, matching exam difficulty. Your Error Log becomes a targeted study guide for the gaps the exam will expose.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes — it runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet, or desktop. No download, no install, no app store. Your progress syncs across devices automatically, so you can start on your phone and finish on your laptop.
How much time do I need per day?
Five minutes is enough. The quest saves your place automatically, and your Error Log builds itself from the questions you miss — no extra study sessions needed. Consistency beats intensity: 10 minutes daily will outperform a 2-hour cram every time.
