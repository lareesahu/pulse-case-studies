01 / The mandateA catalogue, not a collection of claims

Open a gate.
Give people
a way through.

The founder’s brief is to introduce Chinese intellectual heritage through study before judgment. The challenge is one of discovery: texts, people and institutions are difficult to encounter as a connected body of work.

The website gives that material an editorial home and a navigable structure. It is a gateway to further inquiry, not a substitute for scholarship.
