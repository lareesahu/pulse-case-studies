01 / The challengeLearning, without the lecture

Make the next step
feel inviting.

Grammar can feel like a list of things to get wrong. GramLingo frames it as a place to explore: choose a world, take a short phase, work through a question.

The design connects identity, learning structure and interface. The playful exterior has a practical job: help a learner see where to begin and what comes next.

03 / The learning worldsOriginal project artwork

Give an abstract idea
a place to live.

The covers turn grammar categories into recognisable destinations. Their depth, light and character make the lesson menu feel like the beginning of an adventure. These are the original published images—not newly generated substitutes.

04 / Brand behaviourOne character, different moments

A companion,
not a referee.

Gramlin gives the learning journey a familiar face. Thinking, studying and celebrating are different emotional jobs—not interchangeable decorations.

An interactive viewer of supplied poses. It does not simulate a lesson or mark an answer.
