01 / The problemThe distance between strategy and execution

A brand is not
a prompt.

Content work depends on more than a good instruction. It needs a shared understanding of the brand, its audience, its sources and what has actually been approved.

Pulse Growth Engine brings those pieces into an application: brand intake, evidence review, strategy, visual direction, content packages and a review queue. The case focuses on how that workflow is made understandable—not on claims of unlimited automation.
