How this library should be used

Master identity first.
Website evidence second.

The recovered guide governs the portfolio identity until a newer version is confirmed. Current web screenshots show what is actually online; they do not prove that every implementation choice is approved. No public brand centre was found in the official navigation, indexed searches and source files checked. That does not mean a private centre does not exist.

Original client files were not changed. Downloads retain the source bytes. Browser frames are presentation devices; the screens are unretouched captures. No forms, orders or consent choices were submitted.
